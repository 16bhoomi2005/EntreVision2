## PAGE 15–17: WEEKLY REPORT / DISCUSSION RECORD

### Discussion 1: Need Identification & Problem Scope
- Discussed the socio-economic distress in the Katol–Narkhed–Saoner citrus corridor where 25–35% of citrus is lost post-harvest.
- Decided to develop **EntreVision** as a Location-Aware Decision Support System (DSS) integrating agro-climatic, tariff, and mandi datasets with automated bank DPR generation to enable micro-enterprises under the PMFME scheme.

### Discussion 2: System Architecture & Data Engineering
- Designed a 3-tier microservice architecture: Data Tier (PostgreSQL + RAG PDF embeddings), Backend (FastAPI Python), and Frontend (React Vite SPA).
- Collected and cleaned 15 tabular datasets including Kalamna/Nagpur APMC arrivals, MSEDCL commercial power tariffs, and tehsil soil/groundwater profiles.

### Discussion 3: Financial Waterfall & Banking Viability Engine (M3)
- Formalized mathematical algorithms for bank project appraisal following Indian Banks' Association (IBA) norms:
  - Capital Subsidy: $35\%$ of CapEx under PMFME (capped at ₹10,00,000).
  - Promoter Contribution: $10\%-15\%$ own equity margin.
  - Term Loan: Remaining balance amortized over 5 years @ $8.5\%$ interest rate.
  - Cash Flow Modeling: 5-year capacity utilization ramp (60% to 95%) with WDV depreciation (15% machinery, 10% civil) and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) calculations.

### Discussion 4: Agentic RAG Advisor & Text-to-SQL Pipeline (M5)
- Integrated Google Gemini Flash with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over official MIDH and PMFME government operational guidelines.
- Built a Text-to-SQL tool execution agent that translates user questions (e.g. *"What is the distance from Katol to Kalamna?"*) into deterministic PostgreSQL queries to eliminate AI hallucinations.

### Discussion 5: Bank-Grade 11-Chapter DPR Synthesizer & Multi-Page PDF Solution
- Formulated an 11-chapter Detailed Project Report strictly modeled on the official Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) Techno-Economic Feasibility Report standard.
- Integrated `html2pdf.js` for instant one-click A4 client-side PDF downloading and configured print-CSS rules (`.dpr-page-break`, `break-inside: avoid`) to prevent single-page viewport truncation.
- Replaced misleading static license checkmarks with authentic pre-sanction compliance pipeline statuses (FSSAI, Udyam MSME, MPCB Green Consent, GSTIN).
