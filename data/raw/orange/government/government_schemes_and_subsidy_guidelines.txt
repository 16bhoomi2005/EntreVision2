1. PMFME (PRADHAN MANTRI FORMALISATION OF MICRO FOOD PROCESSING ENTERPRISES)
================================================================================
- Department: Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)
- Focus in Nagpur: One District One Product (ODOP) - Citrus / Mandarin Orange
- Pattern of Assistance: 35% Credit-Linked Capital Subsidy on eligible project cost
- Maximum Ceiling: Rs. 10.00 Lakhs per individual micro-enterprise
- Beneficiary Contribution: Minimum 10% of total project cost (equity)
- Loan Requirement: Mandatory commercial bank term loan for the remaining project balance
- Eligibility: Existing and new individual micro food processing units, FPOs, SHGs, and Producer Co-operatives
- Validation Status: VERIFIED

================================================================================
2. MIDH (MISSION FOR INTEGRATED DEVELOPMENT OF HORTICULTURE) / NHB
================================================================================
- Department: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- Applicable Activities:
  a) Commercial Horticulture & Hi-Tech Nurseries (Component SCH002)
  b) Post-Harvest Management, Packhouses & Cold Chain (Component SCH003)
- Pattern of Assistance: Component-specific capital investment subsidy
  - Hi-Tech Nurseries: 50% of cost norm (up to Rs. 12.50 Lakhs/ha)
  - Packhouses / Pre-cooling / Cold Rooms: 35% for general areas, 50% for SC/ST/FPO projects in hilly/scheduled areas
- Technical Standards: Must comply with mandatory NHB/NCCD technical standards
- Validation Status: VERIFIED (NHB MIDH 2025 Guidelines)

================================================================================
3. PMEGP (PRIME MINISTER'S EMPLOYMENT GENERATION PROGRAMME)
================================================================================
- Department: Ministry of MSME / KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission)
- Applicable Activity: Setting up new micro-enterprises in manufacturing or services
- Project Cost Limits: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for Manufacturing, up to Rs. 20 Lakhs for Services
- Margin Money Subsidy Rates:
  - General Category: 15% (Urban) / 25% (Rural) | Own Contribution: 10%
  - Special Category (SC/ST/OBC/Women/Ex-Servicemen): 25% (Urban) / 35% (Rural) | Own Contribution: 5%
- Loan Requirement: Bank loan mandatory for remaining project balance
- Validation Status: VERIFY_REQUIRED (Component-specific verification against latest KVIC portal)

================================================================================
4. PM-KUSUM / MAHAURJA SAUR KRUSHI PUMP YOJANA
================================================================================
- Department: MNRE / Maharashtra Energy Development Agency (MEDA) & Mahavitaran
- Applicable Activity: Off-grid and Grid-connected Solar Agricultural Irrigation Pumps (3 HP to 7.5 HP)
- Financial Assistance Structure:
  - Central Financial Assistance (MNRE): 30%
  - State Government Share (Maharashtra): 60% – 65% (Category-dependent)
  - Farmer / Beneficiary Contribution: 5% (SC/ST) to 10% (General category)
- Validation Status: VERIFY_REQUIRED (Category-specific verification against MEDA state notifications)
