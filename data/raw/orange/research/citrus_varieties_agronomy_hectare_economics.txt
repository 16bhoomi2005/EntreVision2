2. KEY COMMERCIAL CULTIVARS IN INDIA
================================================================================
- Nagpur Mandarin (Citrus reticulata): Central India (Vidarbha, Maharashtra / Chhindwara, MP); most famous Indian mandarin.
- Kinnow: North-West India (Punjab / Rajasthan); high-juice processing hybrid.
- Coorg Mandarin: South India (Karnataka / Tamil Nadu).
- Mosambi (Sweet Orange - Citrus sinensis): Maharashtra; highly popular for fresh juice stalls & commercial juicing.
- Kagzi Lime (Acid Lime - Citrus aurantifolia): Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Rajasthan; most widely cultivated lime.

3. AGRO-CLIMATIC & SITING DECISION REQUIREMENTS (DSS RULES)
================================================================================
Requirement            Optimum Value / Range     Tolerance & Critical Notes
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Temperature            13°C – 37°C               Below -4°C injures young plants & shoots
Soil pH                5.5 – 7.5                 Can tolerate 4.0 – 9.0
Soil Type              Sandy loam to Clay loam   Must be deep, permeable, well-drained
Root Temperature       ~25°C optimum             Optimal root development and nutrient uptake
Humidity               Low humidity              High humidity promotes fungal diseases

CRITICAL SITING CRITERION:
- Citrus is extremely sensitive to waterlogging. Good soil drainage is the #1 mandatory siting requirement.
- Root rot and collar rot (caused by Phytophthora fungi) represent the single biggest disease threat in poorly drained or waterlogged soils.
